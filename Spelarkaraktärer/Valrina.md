## Origin och historia

Decadent. Föddes i en orkan som hennes mor offrades för att mana
fram. Bortbyting, växte upp vid Tritons hov i undervattensriket.

Flydde nyligen från skuggornas dal. Var hon befinner sig nu är
okänt.

## Utseende & personlighet

Uppväxt vid Tritons hov gör att den vanliga, materiella världen
fortfarande känns delvis främmande för henne.

## Bakgrunder

Changeling, Forbidden Knowledge, Sophist.

## Faerie ties (p. 42)

**Elfin secret**. Divine one's most secret desire. If you use this
secret more than once per day, roll your Doom.

**Witchsight**. You can see through the veil that hides the realms
of faerie. Elves, trolls, and other magical beings are always
visible to you. You may be able to see magical auras and detect
illusions with a successful WIS roll.

## Spells (p. 38)

**Animate mirror**. You animate your own reflection in a mirror. It
will attack anyone that passes near it (d6 damage). Lasts until
dispelled or the mirror is destroyed.

**Fading memories**. The target forgets all interactions with you
in the last d6 hours (or years on a roll of 1).

**Greedy hand**. One item the target holds in its hand flies to
yours. On a casting roll of 1, the object is destroyed.

**Unnatural speed**. The target can move to a Far distance on their
Turn. On a 1, a randomly selected piece of the target's equipment is
destroyed.

**Umbral Suggestion**. The caster has defeated her own shadow cast
by a sun in the realm of shadows. She can now command shadows to
perform small actions: grab a leg, scratch a surface, make a
gesture. Like Animate mirror but for shadows.
